Visualising classes

  • Usually gradient ascent: synthesise the image that maximises a class response
    • Start from zeros or small random noise
    • Run it through the network and compute the gradient
    • Update the pixels in the direction that minimises the loss
  • The problem: many arrays of pixels give a very high response, and most of them look like nothing at all
