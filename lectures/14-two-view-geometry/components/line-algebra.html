on the linescalejoinmeet
__EQ_LINEVEC__ __EQ_LINECART__
A line is a triple l = (a, b, c), the same kind of object as a point. A point lies on it exactly when the two dot to zero.
Scaling the triple changes nothing. 2l doubles every dot product, and doubling cannot move where one is zero — so it is the same line.
__EQ_JOINEQ__
The line through two points is their cross product — it must be, since x₁·(x₁×x₂) = 0, and likewise for x₂.
__EQ_MEETEQ__
And the point where two lines meet is their cross product, by the same argument read the other way round.
