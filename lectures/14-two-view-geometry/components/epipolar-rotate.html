Construct a line parallel to OX starting at O′. Because XO is in the coordinate frame of O, we need to rotate XO to get the vector into the coordinate frame of O′.
Rx, x′ and t are all expressed in the coordinate frame of O′, and they all live in the same plane, so t × Rx is perpendicular to the plane:
__EQ_ROTTRIPLE__
This gives us the epipolar line:
