the figureepipolesepipolar linesthe constraint
The same two views of the same point, and the plane they all lie in.
The points where the baseline passes through the image planes are called the epipoles. This is where each camera sees the other one.
The lines through x and e (and x′ and e′) are called the epipolar lines l and l′. They lie in the epipolar plane.
If we know x, then the epipolar line l′ is the set of possible places it can appear in camera O′.

Screen overlap alone is not occlusion — the stretch from a centre out // to its own image point overlaps its card and is in front of it. // // ALL THE SPLITTING IS DONE IN SCREEN SPACE.
